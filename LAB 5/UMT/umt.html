About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, Kuala Terengganu, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct their practical sessions and research.

History and Development

Eventually, the Faculty of Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in Serdang was transferred to Kuala Terengganu, and the branch campus was renamed Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) in June 1996. Later, the cabinet approved the establishment of Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) on 5 May 1999 as an associate campus of UPM.

Kolej Universiti Terengganu was given autonomy on 1 May 2001 and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) on 20 June 2001. On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM was given the status of a full-fledged university and rebranded as Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), focusing heavily on marine science, aquatic resources, and sustainable development.
